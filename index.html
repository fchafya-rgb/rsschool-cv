About

I worked at a college as a programmer-technician and also taught; this proved to be a pivotal stage in my career. Explaining complex concepts in simple terms, structuring information, identifying gaps in someone's understanding, and helping to bridge them—this experience fostered my empathy, patience, and ability to work with people. I believe that a true professional is not only skilled at doing the work but is also capable of explaining it and teaching others.

I always choose the path that requires figuring things out for myself. I didn’t have a ready-made manual when I launched my own order pickup point; I immersed myself in the processes, studied the platforms, and built the logistics and financial accounting systems from scratch. This taught me to adapt quickly, make decisions amidst uncertainty, and not be afrad of making mistakes.

I currently manage marketplace operations and face new challenges every day-from analytics and product assortment to advertising and competition. In every area, I get to the heart of the matter, test strategies, and seek out unconventional solutions.

My greatest strength is that I am not afraid of the unknown. On the contrary, I seek opportunities to apply my skills where there is a need to figure things out, build, and improve. It is important for me to keep moving forward rather than standing still, and I know that the IT field offers this opportunity for endless growth.
